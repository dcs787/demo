03 / The idea

Culture is
our lens.

The visual identity represents the lens through which we see and express our shared values and everyday behaviours.

Our values provide the enduring foundation. Our behaviours bring them to life, moving across and connecting the three Ps.

Like the transparent, overlapping forms of the orb, different perspectives shape a culture that is constantly moving and evolving.

Fluid forms

Organic shapes that move and adapt, expressing the fluid nature of our culture.

Translucency

Overlapping layers bring different perspectives together, creating depth and connection.

Light and materiality

Glass, light and reflection create a lens that shifts how we see and experience things.
